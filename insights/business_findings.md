# E-commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Business Findings and Recommendations

### 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and profitability using Microsoft
Excel, MySQL, Python, and Power BI.

The objective is to understand sales performance, identify profitable
product categories, analyze customer purchasing behavior, and evaluate
business profitability.

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to monitor key business
performance indicators and support data-driven decision-making.

### 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Based on the latest Power BI dashboard:

  KPI                 Value
  --------------- ---------
  Total Sales         2.30M
  Total Profit      286.40K
  Total Orders       5.009K
  Profit Margin      12.47%

### 3. Key Business Findings

#### Finding 1: Overall Sales Performance

-   Total sales reached approximately **2.30M**.
-   Total profit reached approximately **286.40K**.
-   The dashboard displays approximately **5.009K orders**.
-   The overall profit margin is **12.47%**.

**Business recommendation:** Monitor sales and profitability KPIs
regularly to identify growth opportunities and maintain healthy business
performance.

#### Finding 2: Product Category Analysis

The category sales chart indicates the following performance:

1.  Technology generates the highest sales.
2.  Furniture generates the second-highest sales.
3.  Office Supplies generates the third-highest sales.

**Business recommendation:** Prioritize inventory planning and marketing
for high-performing categories. Investigate opportunities to improve
sales in lower-performing categories.

#### Finding 3: Customer Segment Analysis

The sales distribution across customer segments is:

  Customer Segment     Sales Share
  ------------------ -------------
  Consumer                  49.56%
  Corporate                 32.04%
  Home Office               18.40%

**Key observations:**

-   Consumer customers contribute the largest share of sales.
-   Corporate customers contribute the second-largest share.
-   Home Office customers contribute the smallest share.

**Business recommendation:** Develop targeted marketing strategies for
Consumer customers while identifying opportunities to increase sales
from Corporate and Home Office customers.

#### Finding 4: Monthly Sales Trend

-   Sales fluctuate throughout the displayed timeline.
-   Several noticeable sales peaks appear during different periods.
-   Sales performance varies across months.

**Business recommendation:** Analyze high-performing sales periods and
use historical trends to improve inventory management, promotional
campaigns, and sales planning.

#### Finding 5: Customer Performance

-   The dashboard includes a Top Customers by Sales chart.
-   Customer sales contributions vary.
-   Some customers generate visibly higher sales than others.

**Business recommendation:** Identify high-value customers and develop
customer retention strategies to improve long-term customer
relationships.

#### Finding 6: Regional Profitability

-   The dashboard compares profit margin across Central, East, South,
    and West.
-   All four regions currently display a profit margin of **12.47%**.
-   The regional chart also shows equal proportions.

**Business recommendation:** Validate the regional profit margin
calculation and Power BI filter context before drawing conclusions about
regional performance. Additional analysis of total profit by region is
recommended.

### 4. Business Recommendations

1.  Focus on high-performing Technology products.
2.  Improve inventory planning using historical sales trends.
3.  Develop targeted marketing campaigns for different customer
    segments.
4.  Monitor profit margins to maintain healthy profitability.
5.  Identify high-value customers and improve customer retention.
6.  Investigate regional profitability using correctly calculated
    regional KPIs.
7.  Review discount strategies and loss-making products.

### 6. Project Conclusion

The analysis provides an interactive overview of e-commerce sales and
profitability.

The dashboard displays approximately **2.30M in sales**, **286.40K in
profit**, and a **12.47% profit margin**.

Technology is the highest-selling product category, while Consumer
customers contribute the largest share of sales.

The project demonstrates how Excel, MySQL, Python, and Power BI can be
integrated to transform raw business data into meaningful insights and
actionable recommendations.

### 7. Project Limitations

-   KPI values are displayed in rounded units.
-   Regional profit margin values are identical and require further
    validation.
-   Sales performance does not necessarily indicate profitability.
-   Additional product-level cost and discount analysis could improve
    business recommendations.
-   Customer sales contribution does not necessarily represent customer
    profitability.

### 8. Final Outcome

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to analyze sales,
profit, orders, profit margins, product categories, customer segments,
monthly trends, and customer performance.

The project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, SQL
querying, Python-based exploratory data analysis, data modeling,
dashboard development, and business interpretation.
